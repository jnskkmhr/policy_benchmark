Material settings and limits

Stiff cohesive: density 2700 kg/m³, Young’s modulus 4×10¹⁰ Pa, friction 0.839, yield pressure 10⁹ Pa, yield stress 10⁸ Pa, tensile yield ratio 1.
Mild sand: density 1600 kg/m³, modulus 10¹⁵ Pa, friction 0.48, yield pressure 10¹⁵ Pa, no cohesion.
Soft sand: density 1000 kg/m³, modulus 10⁶ Pa, friction 0.30, yield pressure 10¹⁵ Pa, no cohesion.

These are synthetic material presets, not calibrated real materials. Several properties change together. One seed cannot establish a statistical ranking. Solver errors are reported separately from policy terminations.
